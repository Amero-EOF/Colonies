This project is going to be very similar to: https://minecolonies.com/wiki/

The idea of this game is to build up your castle making sure you don't city doesn't die of diseases, you can choose to place your city in different biomes which have different weather events. There are also raids that you have to fend off and all of your gear is produced by the towns people, the entire game is surrounded by the towns people and you essentially being the king ordering them around
